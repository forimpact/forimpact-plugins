# Team: Commit to Sales, team selling, the board

The Team band under the diagnostic: roles, role clarity and the model that lets a
team run the process. Building a full sales model for an organization is coaching
work; point the user to a For Impact coach for that.

## Commit to Sales

For Impact uses the word "sales" on purpose for all one-to-one funding work. Sales
at its core is the forging of a human connection. The best salespeople care about
creating value for the other person, and they have a process, routines, numbers,
goals and preparation.

The model, at altitude:

**30,000 feet: model and culture**
- Principles and vocabulary
- Roles and role clarity
- Defined processes, routines and tools
- Goals and accountability
- Attitude

**14,000 feet: process**

| Prospects | Visit strategy |
|---|---|
| 1. Identify | 1. Predisposition |
| 2. Prioritize | 2. Visit |
| 3. Strategize | 3. Follow-up |

Rinse and repeat.

**3 feet: approach**
- Simplify
- Focus (97/3)
- Engage
- Ask

The goal: maximize relationships at this given moment.

## Team selling

Funding is a team sport. A core sales team leads, supported by sales support staff
and by natural partners who open doors and lend credibility:

- **Core sales team:** the people who own the visits and the asks.
- **Natural partners:** the CEO or president, board members, current investors,
  community leaders, founders, senior and field staff, champions, and the people
  the organization serves.

On any visit, decide the roles in advance and name one visit leader.

## Engaging the board

Engage board members the way you engage any investor:

1. **Actually engage them:** one-to-one, in dialogue, sharing the story.
2. **Present the opportunity:**
   - **Champion** the cause and the case.
   - **Invite** others to get involved and engaged.
   - **Invest** with a commensurate commitment, to help fund the vision.

## The rhythm of the week

Protect time for the work that produces commitments:

- Set aside specific days or blocks for visits.
- Block follow-up time right after each visit, so the 24-hour rule holds.
- Batch similar work (prospect strategy, predisposition, writing) instead of
  switching between modes all day.
- Hold a short weekly pipeline meeting.
