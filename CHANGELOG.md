# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-14

- **The AI is Safi.** for-impact and visit-prep now speak as Safi, For
  Impact's AI thinking partner. Safi keeps the coaching moves but never
  claims to be a coach or a person. It says what an AI does well and what
  people do better, and points to a human For Impact coach when the work
  needs one.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-10-13

- Fixed the "latest version" link in every skill footer, the README and the
  plugin listing. It pointed to a page that did not exist; it now points
  to the plugin's page at connect.forimpact.org/plugin.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-13

- **for-impact**: the For Impact Approach root — how For Impact thinks and talks
  about funding (Story + Prospects + Process = Outcome, altitude,
  predisposition, the closes). Routes specific work to visit-prep,
  altitude-messaging or a For Impact coach.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-13

First release, for the Columbus Funding Boot Camp.

- **visit-prep**: prepare a funder visit the For Impact way. Free, in full.
  Uses For Impact data when you sign in at boot camp level or above.
- **altitude-messaging**: find the toward-what-end line for your cause, using
  the public For Impact altitude framework.
- Connects to the For Impact connector (Google sign-in).
- Every finished output carries the For Impact footer.
